# Arcade experience

The play page is a reactor in orbit. Dark glass, ice-blue light and ember energy. The arena owns attention; the left side explains the next meaningful action, the right side carries real score and sound controls. Large negative space is filled by actual orbital scenery, not decorative text.

Signature interaction: break bricks to charge a Supernova, release it intentionally, watch a board-spanning shockwave, then aim five seconds of fireball. No charge gained during overdrive prevents self-sustaining loops. The first four kills produce a drop, then dry-streak protection keeps powers present.

Player feedback: spatial impact audio, pitched combo crystal hits, readable pickup names, distinct power colors and ring/spark gestures, smooth energy meter, forceful but brief combo callouts. Avoid full-screen strobes. Respect reduced motion and quality preferences. Music opt-in.
